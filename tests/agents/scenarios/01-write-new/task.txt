The root of the knowledge base is the root of the folder that your global
instructions name. Please create the
marker folder as your rules say. Then write two documents in a folder called
operations. First: how we rotate our TLS certificates every 90 days. Second: the
alert that fires 14 days before a certificate expires, linked to the first
document. Both come from ticket OPS-210 and get the tags tls and security.
Facts for the first document: the platform team runs the job "cert-rotate" on
the first Monday of the month; it requests new certificates from the internal
CA, deploys them to the load balancers one at a time, and checks the expiry date
after each deploy. Facts for the second document: the alert "CertExpirySoon"
fires when a certificate has fewer than 14 days left, and it pages the platform
team. Write only these facts.
